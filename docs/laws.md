## What backs each experiment rule?

A **proof** establishes the named proposition for the types and inputs it quantifies over. A **test** exercises concrete implementations, fixtures or files. A **design rule** tells experiment authors what the public API or study protocol expects. The [core statements](../laws/LAWS.bend) give the exact scope; the [proofs](../laws/PROOF.bend) use the same names.

| Rule | What is established | What to check or not assume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Keep world state private. | **Typed interface:** the [engine](../core/engine.bend) passes observations and legal actions to the policy, and a public transition to the learner, not a private-state argument. | A world author chooses the observation. There is no secrecy theorem preventing that author from including private information in it. The [authored-world test](../checks/experiment_checks.mjs) distinguishes reactive and full-state values on the cue world. |
| 2. Reject illegal actions before stepping. | **Proof:** `rejection_preserves_progress` gives the rejected turn no transition, preserves model, score and accepted steps, and records an illegal stop. The engine checks membership in the current legal list before that branch. | This is before the episode-end hook. A training `finish` may still change the model on an illegal exit. The [custom-finish integration test](../checks/experiment_checks.mjs) checks exactly zero transition updates and one finish in that case. |
| 3. Use one transition per accepted training move; freeze evaluation. | **Proof:** `training_uses_one_update` and `evaluation_freezes_model` cover per-transition dispatch. `training_episode_counts_accepted_steps` and `evaluation_episode_freezes_counter` check the whole rollout for the specified counting learner. `evaluation_skips_finish` covers the optional boundary update. | One callback does not mean one weight write: a learner can plan or buffer the event. [Episode checks](../checks/checks.bend) and the [compiled replay and contextual learner tests](../checks/experiment_checks.mjs) exercise both modes. |
| 4. Distinguish terminal, horizon and pause. | **Proof:** `terminal_view_stops`, `terminal_is_never_truncated`, `open_at_limit_is_truncated` and `positive_budget_is_never_truncated` distinguish natural ends from an open view at zero fuel. `pause_composes` proves split budgets give the same cursor; `roll_steps_within_budget` bounds accepted steps. | The property of a cursor in memory is not a filesystem guarantee. The [mid-episode resume test](../checks/experiment_checks.mjs) compares a saved prefix and resumed model with uninterrupted execution. |
| 5. Seed draws and pair arms. | **Implemented and tested:** the ordinary engine mixes separate policy and world draw domains. The [host allocator, `checkSeedBlocks`](../core/experiment_config.mjs) shares a phase/replicate seed block across arms and rejects overlapping or out-of-U32 declared blocks. | [Coin fixtures](../checks/benchmark_checks.bend) exercise the mixed draws; [host tests](../checks/experiment_checks.mjs) reject reused screen, confirmation and assessment seeds. This is neither a proof of statistical independence nor a registry of seeds from unrelated runs. |
| 6. Keep components replaceable and observers non-interfering. | **Typed interface and proofs:** compatible worlds, policies and learners compose through hooks. `observer_preserves_report` proves observing leaves the report unchanged. `history_preserves_legal_actions`, `history_preserves_reward` and `history_reset_starts_with_initial_memory` constrain the supplied history adapter. | An observer sees public decisions and their outcome, not just the action ID. The contextual learner gets the same ordered actions from the source view as the policy; the hook does not take a hidden-state callback. Component types still have to match. The [history reset fixture](../checks/checks.bend) exercises the real adapter; [host tests](../checks/experiment_checks.mjs) reject an incompatible method. |
| 7. Respect next-view semantics. | **Learner-specific proofs:** `terminal_has_no_continuation` covers the Study learner; tile, actor, offline and arena learners have separate terminal laws. `double_q_bootstraps_from_other_table` covers a nonempty legal list; `double_q_left_preserves_right` and `double_q_right_preserves_left` cover the untouched table. `planning_preserves_observed_experience` covers Dyna's stored experience, not all model state. | [Learner fixtures](../checks/study_checks.bend) exercise empty/nonempty Double-Q choices and empirical planning; [benchmark fixtures](../checks/benchmark_checks.bend) distinguish truncation and terminal targets. Arbitrary user learners are not proven to implement these algorithms or to converge. |
| 8. Declare and trace comparisons; confirm on fresh seeds. | **Host-enforced and tested:** the [runner](../core/experiment.mjs) checks plan/checkpoint identity and seed allocation. Its `select` command requires a complete validation-only screen, ranks final validation results and writes a new plan, not trained weights. Running that plan retrains on disjoint seeds. | [Integration tests](../checks/experiment_checks.mjs) cover corrupt checkpoints, frozen replay hashes, validation-only selection and overlap rejection. Choosing a suitable metric and a defensible statistical design remains the author's job; hashes and tests do not prove interval coverage. |
| 9. Keep offline partitions separate and report support. | **Narrow proofs:** `logging_zero_buckets`, `logging_prefers_outside_buckets` and `logging_base_evaluates_at_zero` cover logger choices. `unsupported_action_cannot_enter_backup` is specific to a zero-visit action in the support-restricted backup. | [Logging fixtures](../checks/study_checks.bend) enumerate all 65,536 draws for one three-action setting. The [host tests](../checks/batch_checks.mjs) check additional thresholds, corrupt held-out rows without preventing fitting, reject reused scoring seeds and test missing support. A finite log cannot establish coverage of every reachable action. |

### Other guarantees

- **Sparse updates.** `table_adjust_value` and `table_adjust_visits` cover the addressed observation/action key; the corresponding other-key laws preserve both reads, including absent keys and duplicate input entries.
- **Episode accounting.** `every_report_counts_once` includes illegal exits. The counting-learner rollout laws preserve a signed difference from the starting counter, rather than assuming the counter started at zero.
- **Completion.** `training_finishes_once`, `completed_cursor_never_finishes_twice` and `unfinished_cursor_does_not_finish` cover the explicit finishing hook. `cursor_finishes_as_episode` equates a fresh completed cursor with an ordinary episode for legacy identity-finish hooks. The Monte Carlo self-play law concerns clearing buffered decisions, not resetting the learned table.

The [native runtime spike](../tools/native-spike/README.md) reports sampled CPU/JS replay parity and timings; the supported backend remains JavaScript.
