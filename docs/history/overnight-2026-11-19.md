The sole difference was dry FrozenLake replicate 9: mean return 1 vs 0 over 16 test episodes. Both arms scored zero in every slippery FrozenLake replicate. Its suite probability of improvement against Q-learning was 0.510 (95% interval 0.500–0.530). That suite statistic counts all within-game cross-arm pairs with half-credit for ties; it is not the matched win rate. Neither result establishes a general learner ranking.
